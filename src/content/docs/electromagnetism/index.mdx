These pages begin the NCERT class 12 chapters. A charge here is a body like any in mechanics with one more number,
its charge, and the laws that move it are forces like any other. So everything from class 11 still works: the
free-body diagram, the energy ledger, the graphs, and the textbook answer beside what the engine measures.

Positive charges are drawn red with a plus sign and negative ones blue with a minus sign. An electric field is
drawn as arrows, a magnetic field coming out of the page as dots (the tips of arrows) and one going into it as
crosses (their tails). Move the pointer over the stage in the playground and it reads out the field and the
potential at that point.

## The size of things

The charges on these pages are those of a school laboratory: microcoulombs, on beads of a few grams. The forces
are then millinewtons and the motion takes seconds. An electron in the same fields obeys the same laws about a
billion times faster. The numbers were chosen so that the motion can be watched and the forces read, not because
the physics is any different.

## What is not here yet

Capacitors, circuits, the magnetic field of a current, induction and alternating current are not built. They are
on the [roadmap](/roadmap/).

A few things about what is here are worth knowing before you teach from it.

- **A flat picture has two kinds of magnetic field.** For a moving charge the field points into or out of the
  page, the only direction in which a charge moving in the page stays in it. For bar magnets and compasses the
  field lies in the page. The two are not mixed in one scene, and helical paths, which need both, are not shown.
- **Charges on bodies are points.** A long wire, a large sheet and a spherical shell of charge are there as fixed
  sources, with the fields Gauss's law gives them. A charged rod or disc of finite size is not modelled, and
  plates are drawn round a uniform field, but it is the field that acts.
- **A magnet is two poles.** That is exact outside the magnet and wrong inside it. Iron and other ferromagnets,
  with saturation and hysteresis, are not modelled, and nor is the earth's field as a dipole with dip and
  declination: it is a uniform background.
- **The electromagnetic waves are drawn from their formulas,** like the class 11 waves. Nothing there is found by
  solving Maxwell's equations step by step.
