These pages cover three chapters of NCERT class 12: dual nature of radiation and matter, atoms, and nuclei. They
are in the Modern part of the playground.

Around 1900 three things would not fit the physics of Newton and Maxwell. Light knocked electrons out of metals
in a way no wave could. Atoms gave out light of sharp, fixed colours. And some atoms fell apart at random. The
answers, that light comes in quanta, that particles have wavelengths, and that atoms have a tiny nucleus with
allowed orbits round it, are the subject here.

## What is computed and what is a formula

Where there is something to follow, the engine follows it:

- **Alpha particles** are integrated past a nucleus with Newton's second law and Coulomb's force. Rutherford's
  formula for the angle is not used to draw them. It is what their paths are compared with.
- **Nuclei** decay one at a time by chance, each with the same probability in each instant. The exponential law
  is what a crowd of them does.
- **Photoelectrons** leave the plate one for each photon and are counted as they reach the collector.

The rest is the formulas of Einstein, de Broglie and Bohr evaluated exactly: energies of levels, wavelengths of
lines, the wavelength of an electron.

## Limits

- **Bohr's model is the textbook's.** It gives the right energies for one-electron atoms and is wrong about much
  else: real electrons do not move in circles. The orbits drawn are Bohr's, not nature's.
- **The phototube is a sketch.** Electrons are given every energy up to the maximum equally often, and travel
  straight across. Real ones leave in all directions with a spread set by the metal. Einstein's equation, the
  stopping potential and the threshold do not depend on this.
- **The diffraction pattern is a model.** The angle of the hump comes from de Broglie's wavelength and the
  spacing of the atoms. Its height and width are a simple grating of a few rows, not a calculation of a real
  nickel surface.
- **The binding energy curve is the liquid-drop formula**, a good fit from about calcium upward and poor for the
  lightest nuclei, where the measured values are shown instead.
- **Not built yet:** X-rays, the nuclear force and nuclear radii, alpha, beta and gamma decay as separate
  processes, chain reactions, and semiconductors.
