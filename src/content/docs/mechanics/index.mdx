the equations and the textbook answer. **How the engine does it** says what the computer actually solves and how we

## What makes these simulations trustworthy

- Forces, tensions and normal reactions you see are the numbers the engine used, not drawings added afterwards.
- The energy ledger shows where every joule went: kinetic, potential, dissipated by friction, or added by a push.
- Each scenario is tested against its closed-form answer on every change to the engine. See [Validation](/numerics/validation/).
